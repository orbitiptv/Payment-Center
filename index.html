Frequently asked questions

What should I do if the payment fails?

Try another card, check online payments and available funds, or contact your bank.

How do I send an ABON PIN?

Send the full 16-digit PIN or a clear photo of the voucher through WhatsApp or Telegram.

How long does activation take?

Activation begins after the payment is verified.

Need help with payment?

Send us a message and include a screenshot of the payment or error.
